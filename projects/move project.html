Inescapable roots, rhythmic transits: Navigating the paradoxical cycle of hometown nostalgia and the flâneur's desire to escape.

The Voyage: Deconstructing Dissatisfaction | Hyeonseo Lim | 2023 | video (1m 33s)

Inspired by Charles Baudelaire’s The Voyage and the flâneur's restless spirit, this project begins with a personal dissonance—the chronic dissatisfaction of living in my rural hometown and the simultaneous, burning anticipation of leaving it. Deconstructing the Korean word for dissatisfaction (불만족 / 不滿足), I encountered the paradox embedded in its final character: 족 (足, foot), which historically stems from the pictogram of a foot returning toward a village. Dissatisfaction, then, reveals itself not merely as displeasure, but as an unsettled foot that cannot arrive, rest, or fully settle.

This linguistic and existential tension expands across a series of spatial, material, and visual investigations. From deconstructing my childhood room through collage to interrogating garlic as an inescapable metaphor for local and cultural identity—an indelible scent that clings despite one's urge to flee—the work probes the sticky boundaries of home. The resulting motifs crystallize into the "Lost Wanderer," an avatar born from these fragmented objects, traversing the rhythmic, flickering night lights between my rural origin and the metropolis. Rather than an absolute escape, the journey unfolds as an ongoing dialogue between the gravity of where we come from and the unquenchable drive to drift into the unknown.
