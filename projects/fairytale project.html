Colliding with the crowd, adrift in transit: A contemporary folktale mapping the immutable pulse beneath a fluid self.

Reinterpreting the folktale of the Pied Piper of Hamelin through a deeply personal lens, Place & the Communal approaches perpetual migration and wanderlust (역마살, Yeokmasal) not merely as restless drifting, but as a path to existential self-discovery.

Moving across unfamiliar geographies and colliding with disparate communities, my external identity fluidly shifts and adapts to the collective rhythms around me. Yet beneath this constant friction with the crowd lies a quiet realization: the essence that remains truly immutable is the very spirit of movement itself. Using my own body as the primary storyteller, the digital prints trace an allegorical journey through physical posture and gesture. Staged in the format of a reimagined fairy tale, the work chronicles the collisions, adaptations, and ultimate awakening of a nomadic subject—revealing that coming to know oneself is found not in settling, but in embracing the perpetual current of displacement.

Peeriboo&Boksili | Hyeonseo Lim | 2024 | digital print on paper | 29.7 x 42 cm
