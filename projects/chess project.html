Human & Ai | Hyeonseo Lim | 2024 | ceramic. plastic. food 40x 40 cm

Metabolism versus computation: Staging the fundamental divide between biological life and machine logic across the chessboard.

Biological life is fundamentally defined by the metabolic process—the vulnerable, continuous act of consuming, digesting, and breaking down external matter to sustain existence. The machine, by contrast, operates entirely outside this organic vulnerability; it processes information and calculates decisions without ever having to feed or decay.

Grounded in my daily ritual of playing chess against artificial intelligence, this project translates that cerebral tension into a physical confrontation between contrasting materialities. Replacing conventional chess pieces with food and artificial substances (ceramic and plastic), the work stages a visceral match between human and machine. Here, chess is no longer merely an abstract battle of strategies, but an existential collision between perishable matter that must ingest to survive and an inorganic intelligence that endures without consumption.
