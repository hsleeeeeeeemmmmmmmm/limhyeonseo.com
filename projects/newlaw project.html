1450 | Hyeonseo Lim | 2025 | plastic, sticker, paper, wax, wood | 594 x 841mm

Speculating financial immunity: Confronting systemic currency volatility and the precariat state of international students.

1450 investigates the vulnerability of international students caught in macroeconomic currents beyond their agency. When tuition was originally paid, the exchange rate stood at 1,450 KRW to the Euro; by the time this project unfolded, it had surged to over 1,650—an arbitrary escalation exceeding 15%.

In that period, opening currency charts became a compulsive daily ritual, transmuting academic life into the high-stakes precarity of day trading. This work interrogates the inherent injustice of subjecting student livelihood and educational access to sovereign financial volatility. Utilizing mundane, bureaucratic materials alongside delicate textures—paper, stickers, plastic, wood, and wax—the work crystallizes this psychological strain into a speculative legislative proposal: a regulatory framework to insulate students from volatile exchange fluctuations. _1450_ positions policy not merely as administrative text, but as a protective armor against economic fragility.
