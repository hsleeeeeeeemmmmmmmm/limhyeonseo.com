Berlin City Experiment | Hyeonseo Lim, minjae kim, entong jian, tijmen | 2025 | graphic

Navigating without digital compasses: An urban drift guided solely by human instinct to reclaim the primordial urge to explore.

This project is an urban experiment grounded in radical disorientation. Placed in an unfamiliar city without digital guidance, navigational tools, or a predefined destination, I let movement be dictated entirely by bodily instinct and spatial impulse.

In an era where algorithms optimize our every route and sanitize modern movement, our inherent desire to explore has been replaced by efficiency. Stripping away the scaffolding of contemporary navigation, this journey asks a fundamental question: _Where do we arrive when we surrender direction to pure intuition?_ As each turn was chosen by immediate curiosity—following shadows, textures, and street contours—the city transformed from an engineered grid into an open terrain of chance. Through this intuitive drift, the work documents the tension between urban geography and raw human curiosity, reclaiming the primal act of wandering as a poetic, existential inquiry.
