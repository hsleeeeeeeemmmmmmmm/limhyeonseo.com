The Gospel of Non-Existence: A speculative religion embracing the serene void over the turmoil of being.

Developed within a brief to imagine an alternate utopia, this speculative design project introduces a fictitious religious movement that preaches an eternal return to non-existence. Informed by David Benatar's antinatalist philosophy in _Better Never to Have Been_, the work departs from conventional utopian ideals of abundance. Instead, it posits that the ultimate salvation lies in escaping the perpetual friction between suffering, fleeting pleasure, and existential boredom.

To articulate this philosophy, the project adopts the rhetoric of institutionalized faith, producing a fabricated scripture alongside a persuasive promotional video. The holy text systematizes the ethics of non-being, while the campaign material uses the alluring, polished aesthetic of modern proselytization to market the peaceful silence of the void. By packaging radical philosophical pessimism within familiar media and design formats, the work prompts viewers to question whether existence itself is an unexamined burden and what an ultimate sanctuary might truly look like.
