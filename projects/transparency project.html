Layers of vulnerability on tracing paper: Finding raw honesty within the interstitial gaps of text.

Text possesses a quiet power to disarm the self. Unlike the immediate fullness of visual art, written language carries intervals—interstitial voids between direct words where ambiguity breathes, rendering the interior self undeniably translucent.

Executed on delicate tracing paper, Transparency materializes this tension between emotional restraint and involuntary exposure. As someone who long believed emotions could be calculated, governed, and neatly bound, this work serves as an unraveling. Through a curated weaving of my most primordial, emotionally charged words, the translucent pages layer and bleed into one another. The material acts as both a veil and a lens, exposing the illusion of emotional control and laying bare the raw, unfiltered vulnerabilities that slip through the fissures of language.

Transparency | Hyeonseo Lim | 2025 | publication on tracing paper
