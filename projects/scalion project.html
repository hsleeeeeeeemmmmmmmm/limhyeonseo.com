The scallion became me | Hyeonseo Lim | 2026 | video (3m 24s)

The act of eating: Confronting inherent violence and the metabolic loop of becoming one with the nonhuman.

This project serves as an inquiry into the nonhuman, emerging from a close observation of how I entangle with the material world and how these relations reshape both myself and the other.

At the core of the work lies the simple act of eating a scallion. Through this ingestion, I confront an inherent violence entwined with the contradictory impulse to sustain life. To consume is an act of simultaneous taking and giving—an intimate collision where boundaries blur and existence reveals itself as an unending metabolic loop. In the process of ingestion, the distinction between self and object collapses, arriving at an ecological kinship where I ultimately become one with the scallion.
