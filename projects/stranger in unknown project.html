Stranger in Unknown | Hyeonseo Lim | 2025 | video (7m 30s)

Beyond passive assimilation: Carving out an unrooted, nomadic existence and the pioneering self in an unknown landscape

Arriving as an Asian in an unfamiliar Western city, I found myself navigating a turbulent psychological terrain—oscillating from initial exhilaration to internal conflict, piercing nostalgia, and eventually arriving at a transformed state of pleasure through gradual assimilation. Echoing the narrative tension of _An American in Paris_, this project maps this emotional trajectory through an audiovisual interplay between shifting musical movements and culturally charged objects.

Tangible symbols from both East and West are brought into direct confrontation: they collide, bleed into one another, and are mutually reconstructed as the sonic narrative unfolds. Within this dialogue, boba operates as a distilled metaphor for Eastern identity—undergoing its own process of dislocation and eventual 'integration' into the Western landscape.

Yet this integration is neither a defeat nor a passive submission. Rather than anchoring permanently to a single soil, the work ultimately renders a portrait of the pioneer: an unrooted individual who actively carves out their own habitat amidst displacement, claiming agency over their evolving reality.
