The Zero-Point of Mimicry: From Symbol to Default

This project is a visual and existential investigation that explores the state of “mimicry”—a refusal to be consumed as a specific socio-cultural symbol through a thorough assimilation into my external environment. Confronted by the gaze of others, whether shaped by cultural displacement or an artistic identity, I adopt statistical averageness as a survival strategy to escape the symbols imposed upon me.

In this context, being average is neither an act of blindly following the crowd nor an expression of modesty; rather, it signifies a “default value,” a state of “visual silence” where all discrepancies drop to zero. Immersed within the multi-layered crowd of Amsterdam’s Dam Square, I subtly synchronize my visual codes with prevailing local dress and mass trends. Through a personal archive of various experiments—including an average Dutch face mask and a manual on ‘how to become Dutch’—I document my persistent attempts to disappear into the crowd. Paradoxically, this obsession with flawless camouflage exposes the frustration of an impossible goal, leaving behind a record of ongoing struggle rather than perfect assimilation.
