A playful, tactile instrument: Turning children's touch into a living symphony with nature.

Root the Rhythm_ is an interactive textile installation designed as a tactile, oversized musical toy for children. Blending soft materials with responsive sound technology, the project invites young audiences to explore music through intuitive, bodily engagement rather than structured rules.

Standing at human scale, these soft textile forms respond to hugs, taps, and gentle touches, transforming everyday tactile play into harmonious natural sounds. As children explore the forms together, their collaborative movements generate a layered, ever-evolving melody. _Root the Rhythm_ encourages sensory curiosity and attentive listening, offering children an imaginative playground where they discover that their own playful energy can harmonize with the rhythms of the natural world.

Root the Rhythm | Hyeonseo Lim | 2026 | textile, arduino, banding sensor
