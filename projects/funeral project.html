A ceremonial space for linguistic extinction: Mourning the silenced voices and fragile vitality of lost languages.

Language is not merely a communicative code; it is a living, fragile entity sustained solely through the breath of its speakers. Possessing agency and sovereign power, it survives only through active utterance, mutating and eroding over time. Funeral of a Dead Language constructs a ceremonial sanctuary dedicated to the linguistic ghosts that have quietly expired in this relentless current of change.

Mourning the disappearance of these living vernaculars, the project stages a tactile and spatial funeral. It frames language extinction not as a sterile historical datum, but as the irreversible death of cultural worldviews. By gathering physical remnants and symbolic epitaphs within the space, the installation prompts a shared contemplative reckoning: What fragments of your own dialect or mother tongue have faded into silence? Viewers are invited to step beyond passive remembrance, joining a communal ritual to honor the words that once structured our realities.

Funeral of a Dead Language | Hyeonseo Lim | 2024 | wood, candle, paper, brush, ink | 150x60 cm
